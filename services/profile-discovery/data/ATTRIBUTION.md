# City coordinates

Derived from [GeoNames cities15000](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip), downloaded 11 October 2026. India records only; names and ASCII names retained only when unambiguous, coordinates rounded to four decimal places. Four familiar historical city-name aliases map to current city entries. No person/device coordinates are stored.

GeoNames data is provided under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and [GeoNames terms](https://www.geonames.org/export/). Credit: GeoNames. Data is provided without accuracy/completeness guarantees. Distance is between city centroids, rounded to 10 km in the UI; it is not travel distance or a person's precise position.
